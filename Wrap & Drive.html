FAQ & aftercare

Answers to what customers ask most. Times and lifespans vary by car and material, so confirm yours with us.

How long does a full wrap take?

Usually a few days, depending on the car, the design and how much prep the paint needs.

How long does a wrap last?

Typically several years. Sun exposure, parking and washing habits make the biggest difference.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

On healthy factory paint it protects it. Poorly repainted or damaged paint can lift, so we inspect the car first.

Can the wrap be removed?

Yes. A wrap can be taken off and the original paint is usually intact underneath.

What is the difference between a wrap and PPF?

A wrap changes the look of the car. PPF is a clear self-healing film that protects the paint from chips and scratches. You can also use both.

How do I wash a wrapped car?

Hand wash with a soft mitt and mild soap. Avoid close-range pressure washing at edges, harsh brushes, and wax or polish on matte finishes.

What should I do in the first week?

Keep the car dry and avoid washing for about a week so the film can settle. Park in shade where you can.
